plan the trip, not the panic

THE PROBLEM

Trip planning shouldn't live in eleven browser tabs.

Waypoint helps students and first-time trip planners pick a destination, build a day-by-day plan, and track a budget — all in one place.

FEATURE 1 — ITINERARY BUILDER

Lay out each day, stop by stop.

Pick a day, add a time and an activity, then hit add. You can reorder or delete any stop after it's added.

FEATURE 2 — LIVE BUDGET TRACKER

Watch the total before it watches you.

Set what you can spend, then estimate costs by category. The total updates as you type and warns you if you go over.
